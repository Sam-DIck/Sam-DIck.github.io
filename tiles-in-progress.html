Tiles in Progress

A solo GMTK 2023 game jam project built in 48 hours.

A small game about connected systems

Tiles in Progress was a solo experiment in making a playable idea quickly, then refining the rules until the interactions became satisfying. It is the project I most enjoyed making, particularly because it gave me room to think about how a small set of systems can create meaningful choices.

What I focused on

I handled the design and implementation as a solo developer, including the systems that connect the tile interactions, player decisions, and game flow.
